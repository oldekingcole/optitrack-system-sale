Frequently asked questions

Important transaction details

What is the asking price?

The complete package is offered at $189,900 OBO. Reasonable offers will be evaluated based on price, payment certainty, inspection requirements, timing, and logistics.

Will the system be divided into smaller lots?

The current priority is a complete-system transaction. Requests for smaller quantities may be recorded, but splitting will be considered only if a complete buyer does not materialize.

Can the system be inspected or demonstrated?

Remote verification, in-person inspection, or a mutually acceptable third-party technical inspection may be arranged for qualified buyers.

What licenses are included?

Three USB hardware/security keys are included. Exact products, versions, maintenance status, transfer procedure, and any manufacturer fees are being confirmed and will be documented rather than estimated.

How will shipping work?

Pickup or insured freight can be coordinated based on destination. Pelican cases are included, and a final packing manifest will be available before closing.
