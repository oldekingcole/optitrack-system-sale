Information collected

The inquiry form may collect your name, organization, professional role, email address, phone number, purchasing interest, destination, timeline, proposed terms, intended application, and message.

Purpose

The information is used to evaluate, document, and respond to inquiries concerning the sale of the OptiTrack system; coordinate diligence, inspection, contracting, and logistics; prevent abuse; and maintain transaction records.

Technical information

The inquiry service may process browser and referral information. A salted hash of the submitting network address may be retained temporarily or with the inquiry record for rate limiting and abuse prevention; the system is designed not to intentionally store the raw network address in the inquiry database.

Service providers

Hosting, database, bot-protection, and email-delivery providers may process information on behalf of the seller to operate the inquiry service.
